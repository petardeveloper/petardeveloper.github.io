About the game the long version

The Seventh Circle is a retro movement shooter roguelike. You smash through the hordes of hell in search of a wish, and along the way you get sponsored by patrons of questionable ethics and eldritch origin.

There are multiple champions and patrons, each with their own mechanics, and you can mix and match them. There are passive and active weapons with primary and alt fire, and you can evolve them.

Movement is the core of it. You get very fast.

It's a first person 3D boomer shooter with pixel graphics and a gothic horror look. And yes, Nikola Tesla is playable.

Made in Unity, for Windows. The demo is out now on Steam and the full release is planned for November 2026.

Features

  • Retro movement shooter roguelike, first person 3D
  • Movement is the core, you get very fast
  • Multiple champions and patrons with unique mechanics to mix and match
  • Passive and active weapons with primary and alt fire that you can evolve
  • Pixel graphics, gothic horror, boomer shooter
  • Nikola Tesla is playable
