The Seventh Circle is a first-person roguelike where boomer-shooter movement meets bullet-heaven hordes. You pick a champion, sign a pact with a patron god, and dash, slide and wall-jump through 100 to 200 demons at a time while your build fires on its own.

Every run you collect weapons and passives, evolve them, and pay for everything in blood: it heals you, buys upgrades and gambles at chests. Each patron god sponsors you with passives, curses and castable miracles, from the Military Industrial Complex to Atheism. All of it in a pixel-crunched, gothic PS1-style hell.

Short version: Ultrakill movement meets Vampire Survivors hordes, in a PS1 hell.

Features

  • Quake-style movement: momentum, slides, dashes and wall jumps, with coyote time and speed-loss protection.
  • Hordes: combat tuned for 100 to 200 enemies on screen at once.
  • Roguelike builds: active and passive weapons with primary and alt fire, and weapon evolutions.
  • Patron gods: passives, curses and castable miracles, mixed and matched with your champion.
  • Blood economy: blood heals, buys upgrades and pays for gambling at chests.
  • Style meter, a parry-focused boss, 17 Steam achievements and leaderboards.

About the developer

Inat Studio is Petar Stevović, a solo developer from Belgrade making games in Unity since 2022. He has won first place at two game jams (Pulsar, Engage Gamejam 2026; Petals of Madness, OpenIT gamejam). The Seventh Circle is the studio's first commercial game.
