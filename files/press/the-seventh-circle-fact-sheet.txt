THE PITCH

Ever wanted something so bad you were ready to slaughter hordes of demons for it? Yeah, so does Nikola Tesla apparently...


SHORT DESCRIPTION

Retro movement shooter roguelike in which you smash through the hordes of hell in search of a wish, while getting sponsored by patrons of questionable ethics and eldritch origin.


LONG DESCRIPTION

The Seventh Circle is a retro movement shooter roguelike. You smash through the hordes of hell in search of a wish, and along the way you get sponsored by patrons of questionable ethics and eldritch origin.

There are multiple champions and patrons, each with their own mechanics, and you can mix and match them. There are passive and active weapons with primary and alt fire, and you can evolve them.

Movement is the core of it. You get very fast.

It's a first person 3D boomer shooter with pixel graphics and a gothic horror look. And yes, Nikola Tesla is playable.

Made in Unity, for Windows. The demo is out now on Steam and the full release is planned for November 2026.


FEATURES

ABOUT INAT STUDIO

Inat Studio is Petar, one stubborn guy from Belgrade, Serbia. "Inat" is Serbian for spite, or stubbornness.

The Seventh Circle is the big one. The smaller games are on itch.io, and two of them took first place at their jams: Pulsar at the Engage Gamejam 2026 and Petals of Madness at the OpenIT gamejam. The others are JOvanka JOlic's Bizarre Adventure, Pozoriste Puz and CyberHaze.
